Card Storybook

Unlock chapters per set, claim rewards, and build your legend.

How does Storybook work?

  1. 1. Collect cards per set — your completion % rises automatically.
  2. 2. Unlock chapters — at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% new chapters open.
  3. 3. Claim rewards — open a set and click Claim on available chapters.
  4. 4. Bonus unlocks — some chapters grant extra cosmetics alongside coins.
