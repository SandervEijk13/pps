How does Upgrader work?

Bet Pokécoins and choose a card and spin for your Upgrade.

  1. 50
    1. Choose your Bet Set how many coins you want to bet (min. 10). Higher bet = more target cards available.
  2. 2. Choose a target card Pick a card that is more expensive than your bet. The higher the value, the lower your chance of winning.
  3. 3. Spin the Wheel The Wheel has two sides: WIN (Green) and LOSS (Red). The Size of the sides = your chance to win.
  4. 4. Win or Lose Win: The card goes to your collection. Lose: Your stake is gone — no coins back.
