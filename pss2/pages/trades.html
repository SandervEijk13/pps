    How do Trades work?

    Safely trade cards and Pokécoins with other players all via a clear request.

    1. 1. Put together a trade Choose a player, select what you give and what you want to receive from them (cards and/or Pokécoins).
      (You don't have cards with a lock, but the other player does!)
    2. 2. Request sent The other player receives a popup and also sees the request on this Trades page.
    3. 3. Accept or decline The recipient decides. Upon acceptance, cards and coins are transferred immediately.
    4. 4. Finished! Upon acceptance, the cards will appear in your collections immediately. Rejected trades will be closed.
    Tip You can also trade via someone's profile or the marketplace.
