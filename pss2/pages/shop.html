How does the Pack Shop work?

Buy Pokémon packs with Pokécoins, open them, and build your collection.

  1. buy
    1. Buy a pack Browse the shop and buy packs with your coins. Filter by price to quickly find something within your budget.
  2. 2. Open from inventory Purchased packs go to your Inventory. Open them whenever you want via the inventory button.
  3. 3. View your pulls Each pack reveals cards one by one with an open animation. Click through your pulls.
  4. 4. Saved automatically All cards from a pack are immediately added to your collection. Take your time looking through them and check your review at the end.
