How does the Wheel of Fortune work?

Spin the wheel and win coins, cards, or shop packs.

  1. 1. Choose your payment Spin for 50 Pokécoins or 1 Wheelticket. You can see your tickets in the header next to your balance.
  2. 2. Spin the Wheel Each colored section has its own prize and chance. The wheel stops on one segment — that is your reward.
  3. 3. Receive your prize Possible prices: Pokécoins, individual cards, shop packs or a mega card.
  4. 4. To your account Coins are credited immediately. Cards and packs go to your collection or inventory.
